# Backlink playbook

Packs live in the funnel factory: `docs/backlinks/unleashed-os.md` and `docs/backlinks/courtwise.md` (prospects, angles, draft outreach per site). Read the relevant pack first; update it rather than starting over.

## Method
1. State the linkable asset and why anyone would cite it (data, tool, template, comparison, original opinion).
2. Build 5-10 queries: `<topic> resources`, `best <category> tools`, `<competitor> alternatives`, `<topic> statistics`, `<topic> guide`, `<topic> templates`, `<topic> for <audience>`. Run SERPs (DataForSEO) in batches of at most 10.
3. If competitors are known, check their backlink profile for recurring referrers (if the provider is connected; otherwise "no data").
4. Keep editorial pages: articles, directories, resource pages, comparisons, curated lists. Drop homepages, login pages, thin affiliate pages, spam, direct competitors (unless a valid comparison angle), likely paid placements (flag them).
5. Pick an angle per prospect: broken/missing resource, better current data, useful tool/template, alternative/comparison inclusion, expert quote.
6. Contact discovery from public pages only: author byline, contact page, editorial guidelines, about/team, professional profiles, structured data (`Person`, `sameAs`, `email`). Record only details actually found, with the source URL. Never invent addresses or handles.
7. Draft personalised outreach by page and reason. No mass blasts.

## Local SEO links
Chambers, associations, community pages, supplier/partner pages, directories near the service area; claim and complete the Google Business Profile first (category and claimed status outrank posting cadence). Never recommend review gating, fake reviews or keyword-stuffed business names.

## Rules
- Outreach drafts, DM scripts and prospect lists live in Lead Gen (https://leadgen.unleashed.vision/?view=mkdrafts), never the Factory. Packs in `docs/backlinks/` are research inputs; the sending flow is Lead Gen.
- Sending requires the founder to type the go in the sending chat ("send N" approves the batch).
- Output per prospect: URL | site | type | angle | contact path + source | priority | flags.
